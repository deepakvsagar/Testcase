Common questions

Where are my drafts?

Drafts save automatically on the device you wrote them on. To move a story, open it, go to Finish, and choose Save project file; then use Open a saved project on the other device.

Why can’t I export a video for a long story?

Video export supports stories of about three minutes. Longer stories keep their script, scene plan, voice preview, and project file.

How do I save a video in the iOS app?

After the video renders, tap Download video and choose Save Video in the share sheet.

Dictation isn’t working.

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for StoryVerse in iOS Settings → StoryVerse.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account → Delete account and data. See the privacy policy for details.

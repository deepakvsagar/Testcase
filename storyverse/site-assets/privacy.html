StoryVerse is provided by [MaVir Technologies legal name], [address] (“we”). This policy covers the StoryVerse website and the StoryVerse iOS app.

What stays on your device

Drafts, scripts, scene plans, and settings are saved in your browser or in the app on your device. We don’t receive them unless you ask StoryVerse to generate something. Rendered videos are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

What we process to generate content

When you choose to generate a story, scene plan, scene artwork, voiceover, or video, we send the text needed for that request (your idea, audience, length, mood, story text, and scene directions) to the AI provider for the model you selected: Google (Gemini, Veo), OpenAI, or Anthropic (Claude). They process it under their own terms to return the result. We don’t store your stories on our servers.

Sign-in

The website uses Sign in with ChatGPT. The iOS app uses Sign in with Apple and asks for no name or email; we receive only an anonymous identifier from Apple. We use these identifiers to keep you signed in and to apply short-term usage limits, which are held in memory and discarded within an hour.

Dictation

When you use “Speak my idea” in the app, speech is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition, on your device where supported. We don’t receive audio.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of personal information, and no analytics SDKs.

Children

Bedtime Bestie helps adults create stories for children. StoryVerse is meant to be used by adults; please don’t enter children’s full names, addresses, or other private details.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account → Delete account and data to sign out and erase everything StoryVerse stored on your device. On the web, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. You can stop using your Apple ID with StoryVerse in iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
